## 🎯 Five Core Feature Groups Reference

- **Group 1: Smart Edge Vision & Plate Preprocessing** (Image enhancement, ANPR OCR, RTO compliance checks)
- **Group 2: Multi-Feature Vehicle Tracking (Re-ID)** (Visual signatures: color, type, make, tint, markings, damage)
- **Group 3: Spatio-Temporal Graph Engine** (Directed road graph, travel times, feasible paths, ghost plate detection)
- **Group 4: Law Enforcement & Safety Operations** (Watchlist tracking, stolen vehicle alerts, GIS trajectory history)
- **Group 5: Urban Traffic Flow & City Analytics** (Density, choke points, corridor delays, emergency green corridors)
